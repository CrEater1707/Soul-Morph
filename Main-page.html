Features

🎮 Gameplay

Open world game which has classic and unusual quests, dialogue system and choices that affect the world. At the start, You will have a choice between 10 charachters with different abilities and backstories. So you will be able to play for all of them and you can choose the sequence of the game completion.

⚔️ Fights

Turn-based or real-time combat. There are singleplayer and multiplayer modes. At singleplayer you will play for fun, complete quests, and either fight NPC enemies or find a peaceful way to complete the story. At multiplayer mode you will find a big variety of how to play: if you want to play in a team and to complete a story with friends, you can choose up to 3 people (including you) to help each other with the stories. If you want to mess with other players you can do it in the multiplayer arenas.

🛠️ Craft

Collect resources and create tools and armor with unique properties. Every character will have their own equipment for the story.

🛡️ Clans

Team up in a clan to help each other and earn rewards for conquests!

About game

Soul Morph is an open world game created with love to the genre. There'll be 10 characters, each recieves unique ability and path and world. The worlds and stories are fitted to the character to make every of their abilities useful. You are able to choose the sequence of path completion. So you won't miss anything! The player explores a world full of dangers and secrets, finds allies and fights enemies while leveling up the hero.
